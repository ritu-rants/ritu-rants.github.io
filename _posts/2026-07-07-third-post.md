## The elephant is stuck in the poop hole. Let's get it out.
Perhaps it goes without saying, though I will say it anyways, I understand that I write this post giving a big middle finger to people and their opinions, which is in fact my own opinion. I will give myself a big middle finger too because I am sure that people will also think I am an idiot. You know what, half the time I think I am an idiot. Alas, the point of this blog is not for me to publicly shame people though, but rather, is meant to be a place for me to express my voice. In the words of Mary Oliver, to go "out into the morning and" sing.

## Okay what am I pissed about.

I was never one who has been into watching sports. I don't really know what it was, but they never tickled my brain. To be honest, growing up as a boy, it always felt awkward seeing my friends who really enjoyed keeping up with American football or baseball or whatever, while I just lacked any interest. I wasn't ever teased for it but it was more of a personal faux pas. 

Maybe it was because I was never actually super passionate about playing those sports. I did play soccer growing up, but honestly, did not really enjoy playing that much. What pushes me in this direction of thought leans on my experience with League of Legends. I was addicted to this game as a teenanger, and for the first time in my life, followed the professional league series extremely closely. 

Recently that obsession has been with running. I am currently training for a 50K and in the process of learning about how to train properly became an intense fan of ultrarunning. I had originally been introduced to ultrarunning when I was into thru-hiking, seems that the communities are pretty well connected.

Okay so what pissed me off. Well I was watching the Western States Endurance Run live stream a couple of weeks ago. There are so many stories to be told from the absolute class acts that were running the race this year. The cool weather and the recent advancement in training and fueling theory made this year's race one for the record books. 

The first thing that pissed me off was how absolutely stupid people in the chat were. Firstly, if you have a question about the race, go google holy shit. It is as if people have never had to find information out for themselves. Even if you don't want to google, just scroll up in the damn chat because I assure you that your question was asked five seconds ago. For some reason I find this so incredibly infurating, and I always have. Getting a degree in engineering requires a lot of googling, to put it lightly. There is so much information out there, stop being a dingus and go look for it yourself. Express just the slightest amount of independence.

Second, I was incredibly pissed off about the commentary surrounding the non-binary runners in the race. To have an opinion without being informed on who these athletes are and what they represent is incredibly piss-offable. Not only that, but for your opinion to just being some generic statement peeled and served to you on a MAGA emblazened platter, is quite frankly ridiculous. Why don't you put yourself in the place of some of these athletes and perhaps realize that they are incredibly brave for going to bat like they were. We should not be putting people down, point blank and center. We should be uplifting every who is putting their true selves out for the world to see, because, at the end of the day, we all know that those who put other down are doing it soley because they are afraid that who they truly are will get them nowhere in life.

Third, the fucking morons who felt that they had a place to make commentary about Hans Troyer, who fucking shot for the stars, but got unlucky and DNF'd at mile 70ish. Why don't we just praise the fact that someone tried, and tried in a big way. As someone growing into my role as a full blown adult, I have to be honest, the generations above me have left little in the way of role models. The more I learn, the more I see scummy salesmen dressed up in positions of prestige. It is much more widespread than I had hoped.

What intrigues me about Hans is that it seems he is a person there truly to just send it. To try his hardest and see what he can achieve. To see what dreams he can materialize. Personally, I think it is important to have these people in life who have a dream and go for it. It reminds all of us that we have dreams too and we should also go for them.  

## Fin

Okay that is my rant. Maybe next time WSER should not have a public chat feature. I would be better off not knowing these morons exist.
