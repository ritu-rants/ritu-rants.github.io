subtitle: "a poem for a change"

I tried.

I tried to be still and listen to the birds.

I tried to stop for the moment I have always yearned for.

The moment to let the voice drown away and to be present.

The voices don't stop. They beg for attention.

They say to be productive. To work. To never stop working.

They say I will be rewarded for my hard work.

They say they will go away in the future after I have worked hard enough.

I don't believe them, yet they will not die.

So I still yearn for that moment. 

While I do hear the birds, am I really listening to them? 

Even if not, I won't stop trying.


*— R2*
