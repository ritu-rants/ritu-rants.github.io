## Why blog? Why rant?
I've been thinking about doing this for a while. It is supposed to be a diary, of sorts, where I can share some of my personal thoughts. However, obviously, this is also public, so there are plenty of things that I won't share, though knowing me, that list of things might be pretty small.

Another reason is that I want to get more fluid at writing. One, I hope that this makes me better at expressing and consolidating my thoughts. Two, I want to fend off the temptation to just use an LLM to write everything for me.

## How did "Ritu Rants" come about?

A quick but funny story. From what I recall I was having lunch with a lab mate and I think they were trying to get me to install Goodreads. I just didn't really want to install another social media app on my phone but their argument was that they would love to hear my take on books, apparently I sometimes have meaningful ideas, though I don't necessarily believe that. Either way, the conversation progressed and I mentioned the idea of starting a blog but wanted a comical name for the blog. Their idea was Ritu Rants and here we are.

## What will Ritu rant about?

It is somewhat undecided as of now. I think my goal is just to write at least one of these a week, with the topic being somewhat fluid and decided as I think about it. My plan for next week to is write up some reflections on the end of my Ph.D. and what the job search looked like for me.
